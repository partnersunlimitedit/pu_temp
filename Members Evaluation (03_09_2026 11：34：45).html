Members Evaluation

Annual Members Evaluation Program (EP) 

All members will be objectively evaluated by members of their peers 

Members will be evaluated in the following categories, subject to revision: 

  1. Communication 
  2. Sales activity
  3. Accounting 
  4. Operation efficiency 
  5. Others (to be determined by the members) 
Each category will carry a 10 points value.  Members must maintain a minimum score of 50% efficiency.   Members that does not maintain the minimum score for 3 consecutive years may be asked to leave the network. 

 

Ethics & Code Of Conduct

All members pledged to

  1. Sharing Business within the network with the ultimate goal of 60% 
  2. Members’ responsibility - Protecting the network and the interest of all members.
  3. Sharing resources
  4. Building Trade Lanes mutually 
  5. Treating each other with respect 

 

Debt Protection

PARTNERS UNLIMITED will build up a Debt Protection Fund to cover members for non-disputed nonpaid invoices of a PARTNERS UNLIMITED member.   The Debt Protection Program is compulsory for all members. 

Funds are built up by the members on a yearly contribution basis. 

Details or Application of the rules are defined in An Appendix
