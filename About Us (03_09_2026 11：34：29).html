About Us

Who We Are

We are a group of like-minded mid-size Logistics Providers, forming a network of Partners across the world.

 

What We Believer - Our Core Value

PEOPLE - PEOPLE - PEOPLE

We believe that the most important element is the PEOPLE- our members, we subscribe to
  1. Regardless of the market and size of the company, all members are accepted and treated equally 
  2. The network is about connecting people, connecting the needs
  3. The network must create value for its members 
  4. The best idea on how to operate the network comes from its members.

 

Why We Are

The objectives of the Network are: 

  1. To promote and develop business opportunities among the members for the mutual benefit and protect their mutual interests; 
  2. To enhance the members’ and Association’s market awareness and growth; 
  3. To represent views and interests of the members;
  4. To ensure that services and facilities are provided efficiently and effectively;
  5. To forge a true partnership between the members; 
  6. To offer to members financial security protection plan 
  7. To offer to our members full assistance as needed
